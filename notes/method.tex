{\sffamily\bfseries\Large DK1 imbalance: methods}\par
\href{https://github.com/jacobazu/dk1-imbalance-forecasting}{github.com/jacobazu/dk1-imbalance-forecasting} \textperiodcentered{} October 2026

Forecasts of the chance that DK1 is regulated up, down or not at all in each quarter-hour, and of the spread between the imbalance price and spot. Every three hours for the next four hours, and once a day for tomorrow.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lL@{}}
\toprule
Inputs & Calendar, the spot price, wind and solar forecasts made 48 hours ahead, recent imbalances, and real-time grid data for the next hour. Before the auction the price forecast stands in for spot. \\
Models & Multinomial lasso \citep{tibshirani1996,friedman2010}, XGBoost \citep{chen2016} and their average, refitted weekly. Benchmark: how often each direction happened over the last 28 days. Spread: past spreads at a similar spot price, weighted by the direction probabilities. \\
Scoring & Log loss and pinball loss against the benchmark, Diebold--Mariano tests with a Newey--West variance \citep{diebold1995,newey1987}, calibration, and the value of picking a side \citep{bottieau2020}. \\
Day-ahead to imbalance & Long or short 1\,MW per quarter on the day-ahead auction, settled at the imbalance price, after fees. Compared with always long, always short and the benchmark with Newey--West and Holm. Paper trading. \\
Rules & Each forecast only uses data published when it is made, and the backtest uses the same inputs. Test rules are written before the test. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
